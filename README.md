# 🌎 RecruitVerse

## The Open US IT Recruitment Knowledge Ecosystem

> **RecruitVerse** is an open knowledge ecosystem for US IT Recruiters, Technical Recruiters, Talent Sourcers, Talent Acquisition professionals, recruitment leaders, and modern recruiting practitioners.

**Built by Sachin R — Senior US IT Recruiter | US Technical Recruiter | Technical Talent Acquisition**

RecruitVerse brings together practical knowledge across **candidate sourcing, Boolean Search, Google X-Ray Search, GitHub sourcing, AI Recruiting, recruitment automation, talent intelligence, technical recruiting, US staffing, recruiter tools, screening, training, playbooks, and modern recruitment strategies.**

## 🎯 Mission

> **Make practical recruitment knowledge accessible, searchable, reusable, and continuously improvable.**

RecruitVerse is designed to organize the complete modern recruitment knowledge lifecycle — from discovering candidates to building talent intelligence, applying AI, improving recruiter workflows, and sharing proven sourcing techniques.
